## matrix-reasoning-generator

Source: https://github.com/jakopx-berg/matrix-reasoning-generator

Adapted rule-generation concepts and selected matrix construction logic for the
Brain Lab visual matrix practice generator. Puzzle rendering, React integration,
difficulty selection, answer choices, and tests were implemented for Sahadhyayi.
